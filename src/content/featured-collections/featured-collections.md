featuredCollectionList:
  - name: Spells of Genesis
    description: >-
      Spells of Genesis (SoG) is the first game in history to feature cryptographic tokenized assets. 
      With over 280+ cards included, players enjoy unprecedented freedom to play, trade, collect and sell with full autonomy over their in-game cards. 
      The SoG ecosystem (created by EverdreamSoft) is vast and includes the BITCRYSTALS currency, the first tokenized in-game asset (FDCARD), 
      crossover tokens like SATOSHICARD and SARUTOBICARD, and many other projects released in the contemporary era. 
      Year: 2015. Company: https://everdreamsoft.com/ Explore: https://orbexplorer.com/collection/eSog
    link: "https://spellsofgenesis.com/"
    logo: /images/featured-collections/spells-of-genesis.png
  - name: Rare Pepe
    description: >-
      The Rare Pepe Directory is considered an integral part of the birth of the global cryptoart 
      movement as it was the first curated project to invite any artist from anywhere in the world to participate. 
      The project centers around a central theme of an internet meme called Pepe. 
      These cards were (and still are) traded freely using Counterparty functionality, though submissions were closed in 2018. 
      There is a significant amount of culture, Counterparty firsts, and deep lore from this project. 
      Year: 2016. Explore: https://pepe.wtf/ Archive: https://web.archive.org/web/20191105144553/https://bitcointalk.org/index.php?topic=1617264.0
    link: "http://rarepepedirectory.com/"
    logo: /images/featured-collections/rare-pepe.png
  - name: Bitcoin Stamps
    description: >-
      Bitcoin Stamps is an on-chain, immutable digital art protocol on Bitcoin pioneered by Mike In Space, with implementation assistance by Kevin and Arwyn. 
      It leverages a bare-multisig technique on Numeric Counterparty assets to store data permanently, bypassing OP_RETURN limitations, with an OLGA encoding method added later. 
      Since launching in 2023, the protocol has seen over 1.7 million stamps minted, along with a growing ecosystem of art collections and artists. 
      The SRC-20 standard was later introduced to enable fungible tokens with rich metadata. 
      Year: 2015 Explore: https://stamped.ninja/ Spec: https://github.com/mikeinspace/stamps/blob/main/BitcoinStamps.md
    link: "https://stampchain.io/"
    logo: /images/featured-collections/bitcoin-stamps.png
  - name: Fake Rares
    description: >-
      After the closing of the original Rare Pepe directory in 2018, many rejected assets existed and many artists remained without a place to submit their work. 
      Started by Scrilla, this project includes a directory of historic rejected tokens (Series 0), an open-arms main submissions directory, and a more accessible commons directory. 
      Fake Rares remains the longest-running open-submission crypto art directory in the ecosystem. 
      Year: 2021. Explore: https://pepe.wtf/collection/Fake-Rares
    link: "https://fakeraredirectory.com/"
    logo: /images/featured-collections/fake-rares.png
